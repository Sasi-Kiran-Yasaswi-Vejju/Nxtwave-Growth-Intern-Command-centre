# NxtWave AI Workshop Growth Challenge: Campaign Strategy & Execution Playbook

## 1. Executive Strategy Summary
- **Workshop Name**: "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"
- **Target Audience**: 500 Final-Year Engineering Students (2027 Passing Out across CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mech, Civil)
- **Primary Objective**: Acquire 500 qualified registrations within a 7-day sprint on a strict ₹2,000 budget.
- **Operating Framework**: `IDEA → BUILD → LAUNCH → MEASURE → LEARN → SCALE`

---

## 2. Core Positioning & The Value Proposition Pivot

### The Problem with Status Quo Messaging:
Most student campaigns use generic statements like *"AI is the future of work"* or *"Learn Generative AI with Industry Experts"*. In testing, this creates lukewarm interest because students are already inundated with free YouTube tutorials, college webinars, and academic courses.

### The Winning Psychological Hook:
> **"Build an actual AI project in 60 minutes and leave with a live deployed URL, verified GitHub commits, and interview talking points you can defend in your next campus placement drive."**

### Why Final-Year Engineering Students Convert on This Hook:
1. **Urgency**: Campus placement season is either currently happening or weeks away.
2. **Fear of Sameness**: Over 90% of applicants submit identical Titanic or Iris classification projects.
3. **Time Scarcity**: Engineering students have final semester project reviews and exams; promising a complete, deployed build in **60 minutes** drastically reduces perceived effort.
4. **Zero Hardware Barrier**: Running in Google Colab / cloud APIs removes local CUDA/GPU errors that typically frustrate students.

| **5. Targeted Micro-Paid Experiment** | 25 | 5% | ₹1,200 | ₹400 | ₹28.57 | High-intent Instagram Story ads targeted to Tier-2/3 college pins |
| **TOTALS** | **500** | **100%** | **₹2,000** | **₹850** | **₹2.60** | **Blended Unit Economics** |

### Why We Did NOT Spend ₹2,000 on Paid Ads:
A novice marketing approach assumes that ₹2,000 should be placed into Facebook/Google ads. However:
- The standard Cost Per Click (CPC) for Indian engineering students on Meta is ₹6 to ₹14.
- At an industry-average 10% landing page conversion rate, each registration costs ₹80 to ₹120.
- ₹2,000 in ads would yield only 16 to 25 registrations (failing the 500 target by 95%!).
- **Growth Philosophy**: We reserved paid ads as a controlled micro-experiment to identify high-performing creative messaging, while driving 95% of registrations through organic community loops and viral referral mechanics.

## 4. Channel Deep-Dives

### Channel 1: College WhatsApp Communities (Planned: 200 | Day 5: 138)
- **Target**: Placement preparation groups, department unofficial groups, coding club chats across 35 engineering colleges.
- **Message Architecture (Winning Variant B)**:
  ```
  🚨 Final-Year Placement Alert (2027 Passing Out)

  Recruiters spend <15 seconds on your resume. If you still have "Iris Dataset" or "Todo App", your resume gets skipped.

  On Sunday at 7 PM, build and deploy a real AI project in 60 minutes:
  ✅ 1 Live Deployed URL (Streamlit/FastAPI)
  ✅ 1 Clean GitHub Repository with verified commits
  ✅ 3 Exact Resume Bullet Points for Tech Interviews
  ✅ 100% Free + No Heavy Laptop GPU needed

  Seats limited to 500 to keep live mentor rooms interactive:
  👉 Claim your seat here: [LINK]
  ```

### Channel 2: Viral Student Referral Engine (Planned: 150 | Day 5: 104)
- **Viral Mechanics**: Every registered student receives a unique referral pass (e.g. `YASASWI42`) and full share link.
- **Incentive Structure**:
  - **1 Friend**: Unlocks *AI Placement Resume Bullet Bank* (10 tested ATS-compliant bullets).
  - **3 Friends**: Unlocks *Production GitHub Starter Kit* (FastAPI + LangChain + Docker setup).
  - **5 Friends**: Unlocks *VIP 1-on-1 AI Resume Review + Front Row AMA with NxtWave Tech Leads*.
- **Attribution & Transparency**: Real-time counter and live leaderboard displaying top campus ambassadors.

### Channel 3: Campus Tech Clubs & Student Leads (Planned: 75 | Day 5: 46)
- **Target**: Outreach to 15 student leads (GDSC Leads, CSI heads, placement student coordinators).
- **Incentive**: Free NxtWave AI Certificate of Campus Partnership + Priority access to future hackathons for their club members.
- **Spend**: ₹300 allocated for minor digital recognition perks.

### Channel 4: Organic Social & LinkedIn Tech Content (Planned: 50 | Day 5: 25)
- **Content Hooks**:
  - Carousel: *"The 3 AI projects that instantly get tech recruiters' attention (and 3 you should delete today)"*.
  - Student poll: *"Which AI architecture does your dream company ask about: RAG, Fine-Tuning, or Agents?"*
  - Direct call-to-action to use the free **AI Project Idea Generator**.

### Channel 5: Targeted Micro-Paid Experiment (Planned: 25 | Day 5: 14)
- **Objective**: Controlled testing of ad creatives to validate messaging resonance before scaling.
- **Targeting**: Meta Ads Manager -> Ages 20–23, Field of Study: Computer Science / Engineering, Geography: Tier-2 engineering hubs (Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Pune, Indore, Warangal).
- **Ad Creative**: Short 15s screen recording of generating a project blueprint in 10 seconds and deploying it.
