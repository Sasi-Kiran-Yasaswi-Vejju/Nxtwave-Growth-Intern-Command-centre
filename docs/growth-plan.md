## Part 1: Executive 2-Page Growth Plan

### 1. Target Audience & Student Insight
- **Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: Final-year engineering students (Batch 2025/2026 across CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil) preparing for on-campus and off-campus placements.
- **Core Frustration & Pain Point**:
  - The "Resume Duplication Crisis": Over 90% of engineering student resumes contain the exact same copied tutorial projects (Iris flower classification, Titanic survival prediction, or basic Todo apps).
  - Hiring managers and tech recruiters review candidate resumes in under 15 seconds and instantly discount these generic projects.
  - Students know Generative AI is crucial for modern tech interviews, but face high friction (unclear LLM setup, paid API key barriers, complicated vector DBs, and lack of guided deployment).
- **The Value Proposition Pivot**:
  - *Previous generic messaging*: "Learn AI in 60 Minutes." (Weak differentiation, sounds like another boring lecture).
  - *Winning high-intent messaging*: **"Build an actual AI project in 60 minutes and leave with a live deployed URL, GitHub commits, and talking points you can defend in your next placement interview."**
- **The Psychological Hook**: Zero-friction setup (runs in cloud GPU Colab environments, no local installation headaches) + immediate tangible portfolio artifact.

### 3. The 7-Day Sprint Execution Playbook

- **Day 1: Foundation & Telemetry Launch**
  - Ship full web application (Landing Page + AI Project Generator + Referral Tracker + Analytics).
  - Instrument 5-stage funnel telemetry (`page_view` → `generator_run` → `form_start` → `registration_success` → `share_click`).
  - Deploy initial seeded seed list of 8 campus tech club presidents across Hyderabad, Bangalore, and Chennai.
- **Day 2: Community Distribution & Copy Experiment**
  - Distribute Variant A vs Variant B messages across 30 engineering WhatsApp groups.
  - Discover that short 4-bullet format with placement hooks delivers 2.2x CTR compared to long descriptive text. Standardize short copy.
- **Day 3: Mid-Funnel Optimization & Drop-off Fix**
  - Analytics show a 50.8% drop-off from AI Generator usage to registration form.
  - Implement 1-click bridge button: "Build This In Workshop" that automatically transfers the generated project title and branch into the registration form.
  - Form completion rate increases by +18.4%.
- **Day 4: Ambassador Milestone Push**
  - Launch A/B test on referral milestones: 5 referrals vs 3 referrals for GitHub Starter Kit.
  - Lowering to 3 friends unlocks 2.25x viral referral generation ($K$-factor jumps from 0.21 to 0.46).
- **Day 5: Leaderboard Gamification & Status Check**
  - Top referrers recognized publicly on the live Campus Leaderboard (Top lead Yasaswi Sharma hits 27 referrals).
  - Campaign reaches 327 confirmed registrations (65.4% of 500 target), with blended CAC at an ultra-low ₹2.60.
- **Day 6: Urgency & Scarcity Push**
  - Trigger "Last 100 Seats" banner and deploy ₹400 targeted retargeting ads to students who dropped off after using the generator.
  - Run WhatsApp T-48h workshop preview broadcast.
- **Day 7: Final Sprint & Synthesis**
  - Push final 60 seats via campus ambassador peer networks.
  - Hit 500 projected registrations milestone.
  - Synthesize campaign analytics, evaluate CAC, and document playbook for repeatable scaling.
