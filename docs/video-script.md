# NxtWave AI Workshop Growth Challenge: 3-Minute Video Walkthrough Script

**Speaker Persona**: Growth Intern Candidate (Growth Associate / Product Builder)  
**Tone**: Direct, energetic, data-driven, startup-minded  
**Target Duration**: Exactly 180 Seconds (3 Minutes)

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] 1. The Problem & Challenge Context
*(Visual: On camera or screen recording showing the project repository header)*

> "Hi everyone! For the NxtWave Growth Challenge, the mission was straightforward but tough: get 500 final-year engineering students to register for a free online workshop called **'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'**—with only a ₹2,000 budget and 7 days.
> 
> The easiest mistake here is to spend that ₹2,000 on generic Meta ads or just build a basic landing page. But with average student ad CACs around ₹100, ads alone would barely get us 20 registrations. We needed a real growth engine."

---

### [0:20 – 0:45] 2. Target Audience & The Core Insight
*(Visual: Showing the problem statement and placement bullet points on screen)*

> "So we started by looking closely at our audience: final-year engineering students preparing for campus placements.
> 
> The core insight? Students don't need another generic theory webinar. Over 90% of resumes have the exact same Iris dataset or Todo list, and recruiters skip them in ten seconds.
> 
> Our value proposition wasn't 'Learn AI'—it was:
> **'Build an actual AI project in 60 minutes and leave with a live deployed URL, GitHub commits, and talking points you can defend in your next interview.'** That single shift changes conversion completely."

---

### [0:45 – 1:20] 3. The Growth Strategy & Channel Architecture
*(Visual: Showing the channel breakdown table and 7-day sprint)*

> "To hit 500 registrations on ₹2,000, we built a capital-efficient acquisition model across 5 channels:
> 
> First, **College WhatsApp Communities**—driving 200 registrations at ₹0 cost through concise, 4-bullet messages across 35 campus batches.
> 
> Second, our **Student Referral Engine**—driving 150 registrations by turning every registered student into an ambassador with unique referral codes.
> 
> Third, **Campus Tech Clubs & Student Leads**—partnering with GDSC and CSI leads for 75 registrations.
> 
> Fourth, **Organic LinkedIn Content**—before/after resume tear-downs for 50 registrations.
> 
> And fifth, a **₹1,200 Micro-Paid Experiment** on Instagram stories—yielding 25 registrations while giving us real ad creative data.
> 
> Our blended CAC came out to just **₹2.60 per student**."

---

### [1:20 – 2:15] 4. Live Working Asset Demo
*(Visual: Screen share navigating the live application)*

> "Now let's look at what I built. Rather than a static landing page, I built the **AI Workshop Growth Engine**.
> 
> *(Clicking into AI Generator)*
> First, the **AI Project Idea Generator**. A student selects their branch—say, Computer Science or ECE—their interest area like FinTech or Healthcare, and their skill level. With one click, the system generates an interview-ready project blueprint, a 60-minute build roadmap, and an ATS-ready resume bullet point. This proves value before asking for contact info.
> 
> *(Clicking 'Build This in Workshop' → Form fills)*
> When they click 'Build This in Workshop', the details automatically pre-fill into our frictionless registration form.
> 
> *(Submitting registration)*
> Once registered, boom: the viral referral loop activates. The student gets a personalized referral code—like `YASASWI42`—and a 1-click WhatsApp share button.
> 
> As they refer friends, they unlock placement resources: 1 referral unlocks our Resume Bullet Bank; 3 referrals unlocks a VIP GitHub Starter Kit; and 5 unlocks a 1-on-1 resume review.
> 
> *(Navigating to Campus Leaderboard and Analytics Dashboard)*
> We also have a live **Campus Leaderboard** showing top referrers, and a comprehensive **Growth Analytics Dashboard** tracking our 5-stage funnel, daily pacing curve, channel attribution, and A/B experiments."

---

### [2:15 – 2:40] 5. The 500-Registration Mathematical Model
*(Visual: Highlighting the KPI cards and funnel metrics on the Dashboard)*

> "Here's the math behind hitting 500:
> With 1,840 landing visitors and our interactive generator, our visitor-to-registration conversion reached 24.8%, delivering 327 registrations by Day 5.
> 
> More importantly, 56.9% of registered students shared their link, producing a viral coefficient ($K$) of 0.46. That viral referral tail accounts for nearly a third of all registrations without spending an extra rupee."

---

### [2:40 – 3:00] 6. What I Learned & What AI Suggested That I Rejected
*(Visual: Back to camera / concluding summary)*

> "Finally, how we used AI:
> AI initially suggested spending 80% of our budget on search ads, and offering cash prizes for referrals. I rejected both. Cash ruins budget economics and invites spam; while high-value educational assets like GitHub starter kits cost nothing and attract high-intent students.
> 
> If I had another 24 hours, I'd hook up live WhatsApp Cloud API webhooks for instant delivery and build referral fraud deduplication.
> 
> This project shows how I work: **Idea, Build, Launch, Measure, Learn, and Scale.** Thank you!"
