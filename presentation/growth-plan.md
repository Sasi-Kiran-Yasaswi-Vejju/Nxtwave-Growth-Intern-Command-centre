SLIDE 1: STUDENT INSIGHT & VALUE PROPOSITION
================================================================================
CAMPAIGN: "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"
TARGET: 500 Final-Year Engineering Students | BUDGET: ₹2,000 | TIME: 7 Days

THE CORE STUDENT PAIN:
• The Placement Resume Crisis: Over 90% of final-year students put copy-pasted tutorial
  projects on their resumes (Iris classification, Titanic dataset, To-Do lists).
• Tech recruiters review resumes in <15 seconds; generic projects are skipped immediately.
• Students want Generative AI experience, but face high barriers (GPU setup, paid API keys).

THE VALUE PIVOT:
• Generic (Rejected): "Learn AI in 60 Minutes."
• High-Intent (Winning): "Stop Listing 'Basic Python' on Your Resume.
  Build a Real AI Project in 60 Minutes with Live Deployed URL & Interview Talking Points."

KEY PSYCHOLOGICAL HOOK:
• 60-Minute Time-Box: Zero procrastination.
• Zero Hardware Friction: Runs on Google Colab / cloud GPUs.
• Direct ROI: Immediate interview differentiation.

SLIDE 2: 5-CHANNEL DISTRIBUTION & BUDGET ALLOCATION
================================================================================
TOTAL BUDGET: ₹2,000 | STRATEGY: Organic Community Leverage + Viral Referral Loop

WHY WE REJECTED HEAVY AD SPEND:
• Cold ads cost ₹80–₹120 per registration. ₹2,000 would buy barely 20 registrations.
• We reserved ₹1,200 for micro-experiments & deployed ₹800 into organic community leverage.

   • Partnering with GDSC, CSI, and ISTE campus leads with partner certificates.

   • "Before/After" resume teardown carousels and placement project comparisons.

   • Controlled Instagram Story testing targeting Tier-2 engineering colleges to measure cold CAC.
